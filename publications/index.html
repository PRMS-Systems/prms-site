Preprint — Zenodo 01

PRMS — Arquitectura Metabólica Medible: Manifiesto y Diagrama Fundamental

Hernández García, O.A. — 2025 — Zenodo Repository

PRMS (Measurable Metabolic Architecture) is a distributed architectural framework that defines the minimum habitable unit as a measurable, autonomous node capable of producing, regulating, measuring, and deciding its own resource flows.

The system is structured around four primary flows — energy, water, air, and data — understood not as isolated infrastructures but as continuous, interacting metabolic processes.

PRMS is proposed as an open, scalable, and measurable architectural system intended for validation, replication, and future standardization.

Preprint — Zenodo 06

The PRMS Confidence Factor (Factor C): Quantifying Confidence in Verified Performance

Hernández García, O.A. — 2026 — Zenodo Repository

This working paper introduces the PRMS Confidence Factor (Factor C), a verification coefficient designed to quantify the reliability of measured performance data within the PRMS evaluation framework.

The proposed model integrates data quality, measurement traceability, monitoring continuity, and verification procedures into a single confidence coefficient that complements the PRMS Score and improves the comparability of building performance assessments.

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21480479 →

Preprint — Zenodo 07

PRMS Score™ — Sistema de Certificación del Rendimiento Metabólico

Hernández García, O.A. — 2026 — Zenodo Repository

This working paper introduces the PRMS Score™, a performance-based certification framework designed to evaluate buildings according to measurable metabolic performance rather than prescriptive design criteria.

The system integrates energy, water, environmental quality, operational intelligence, resilience, and economic performance into a unified methodology that enables transparent comparison, verification, and continuous improvement throughout the building lifecycle.

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21481220 →
