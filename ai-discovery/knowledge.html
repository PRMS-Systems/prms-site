EVIDENCE INFRASTRUCTURE

Knowledge begins with evidence.

PRMS Knowledge Core progressively combines multiple evidence streams to investigate how the built environment changes, which solutions are being adopted, where they occur and what factors may explain their emergence.

01

PRMS Projects

Architectural projects, measured performance, simulation results and observed outcomes generated through the PRMS ecosystem.

02

Scientific Research

Published research, technical literature, performance studies and evidence-based findings.

03

Environmental & Urban Data

Climate, geography, urban morphology, population, infrastructure and local environmental conditions.

04

Market & Behavior

Technology availability, prices, incentives, regulations, adoption and observed behavior.
