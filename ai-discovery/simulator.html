    DECISION LOGIC

    From project model to architectural knowledge

    PRMS Simulator does not stop at a scenario score. Its intended research loop is: establish evidence → model relationships → simulate alternatives → compare consequences → detect patterns → formulate a conclusion → generate a report → interrogate the simulated world.

    Observe

    Collect architectural, environmental, human and contextual variables.

    Model

    Represent the project as a connected knowledge structure.

    Simulate

    Test alternative intervention pathways and estimate consequences.

    Learn

    Search for patterns, expose uncertainty and identify the next evidence required.

    PRMS Knowledge Core

    Simulation results can become evidence for the independent PRMS Knowledge Core, where patterns can later be compared across projects and contexts.
