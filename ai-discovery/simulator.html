DECISION LOGIC

From diagnosis to decision

PRMS Simulator is designed to compare alternative architectural strategies rather than produce a single deterministic answer. Each scenario represents a different intervention pathway and its predicted consequences.

Diagnose

Establish the baseline condition and identify the principal sources of uncertainty.

Simulate

Test alternative architectural interventions and estimate their performance consequences.

Compare

Evaluate multiple scenarios across energy, water, comfort, carbon, economy and overall PRMS performance.

Decide

Identify the most appropriate strategy according to project objectives, constraints and evidence.
