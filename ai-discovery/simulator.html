Scenario strategies

Option A — Passive

Architecture-led intervention

Improve building performance primarily through architectural and passive environmental strategies.

  • Improved envelope
  • Solar protection
  • Orientation response
  • Natural ventilation
  • Reduced cooling demand

Option B — Integrated

Architecture + efficient systems

Combine passive strategies, efficient building systems and renewable generation to balance performance and investment.

  • High-performance envelope
  • Efficient HVAC
  • Photovoltaic generation
  • Solar thermal
  • Water efficiency

Option C — Metabolic

Integrated performance + resource autonomy

Maximize environmental performance and resource autonomy through an integrated metabolic strategy.

  • Advanced envelope
  • Passive + active systems
  • Renewable generation
  • Water recovery and reuse
  • Storage and adaptive operation

DECISION LOGIC

From diagnosis to decision

PRMS Simulator compares alternative architectural strategies rather than producing a single deterministic answer. Each scenario represents a different intervention pathway and its predicted consequences.

Assess

Establish the measured baseline condition and identify the principal sources of uncertainty.

Simulate

Test alternative architectural interventions and estimate their performance consequences.

Compare

Evaluate multiple scenarios across energy, water, comfort, carbon, resource autonomy, economy and overall PRMS performance.

Decide

Identify the most appropriate strategy according to project objectives, constraints and available evidence.
