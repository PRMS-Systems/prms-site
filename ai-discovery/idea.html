System architecture

Six intelligence engines.

Conceptual components of a future technology discovery environment. Each engine performs a distinct role within one continuous research system.

01 — KNOWLEDGE ENGINE
Knowledge Engine

Scientific literature, patents, technical documentation, datasets and structured knowledge.

02 — RESEARCH ENGINE
Research Engine

Explores the state of the art, relationships, contradictions, limitations and knowledge gaps.

03 — HYPOTHESIS ENGINE
Hypothesis Engine

Generates, compares, critiques and tracks explicit hypotheses and their supporting evidence.

04 — DESIGN ENGINE
Design Engine

Transforms hypotheses into concepts, architectures, parameters and alternative technological solutions.

05 — SIMULATION & EXPERIMENT
Simulation & Experiment Engine

Designs computational simulations and experiments to test competing hypotheses.

06 — KNOWLEDGE EVOLUTION
Knowledge Evolution Engine

Converts validated results into structured, reusable knowledge and feeds future discovery cycles.

Evidence model

Evidence before conclusions.

A discovery system must distinguish what is known from what is inferred. Conclusions should remain traceable to their evidence and method of verification.

Discovery is not a conversation. It is a structured research process.

The conversation can be an interface. It is not the underlying unit of knowledge. The Discovery Project preserves the evolving state of a technological investigation and creates a traceable bridge between ideas, evidence, hypotheses, experiments and results.
