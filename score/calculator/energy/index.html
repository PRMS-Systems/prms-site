Energy Performance Module

D1 Energy evaluates how the project produces, consumes, exchanges, and manages electricity. This dimension measures photovoltaic generation, annual electricity consumption, grid import/export, installed photovoltaic capacity, energy autonomy, self-consumption, and energy intensity.

The objective is to determine whether the building behaves as a passive consumer, a partially self-sufficient asset, or an active metabolic energy node.
