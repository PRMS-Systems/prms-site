Water Performance Module

D2 Water evaluates how the project consumes, captures, reuses, and discharges water. This dimension measures annual potable water consumption, rainwater capture, greywater reuse, sanitary discharge, own water coverage, and reduction against baseline consumption.

The objective is to determine whether the building depends entirely on external supply or behaves as a partially autonomous water metabolism with capture, reuse, and demand reduction.
