Resilience Performance Module

D6 Resilience evaluates the capacity of the project to maintain operational continuity under scarcity, outages, system failure, or extreme conditions. This dimension measures electrical autonomy, water autonomy, redundancy of critical systems, annual continuity, recovery time, and active energy sources.

The objective is to determine whether the building can continue operating as a metabolic system when external infrastructure becomes unstable or unavailable.
