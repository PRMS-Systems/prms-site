PRMS Score
Calculator

The PRMS Score Calculator is the operational implementation of the PRMS Score framework. It allows a project to be evaluated through measured values, weighted indicators, verification levels, and metabolic performance dimensions.

Before entering data

This calculator is structured as a step-by-step evaluation sequence. Each project must first define its general information, audit level, and evidence type. Then, each of the eight PRMS dimensions is completed using measured or documented values.

The objective is not only to obtain a numerical score, but to understand how the building performs as a metabolic system: how it produces, consumes, reuses, stores, verifies, and manages critical flows over time.

Current status

Beta validation version. The calculator is currently being tested through case studies, including SmartLiving Milenio III in Querétaro, Mexico.

General Project Data

Start by entering the core project information. These fields define the evaluation context and are used to generate the final PRMS Score report.

Project Code

Unique PRMS project identification code.

Project Name

Name of the building or evaluated system.

Owner

Individual, organization, or entity responsible for the project.

Typology

Residential, retrofit, multifamily, small commercial, or other PRMS typology.

Climate Zone

Climate classification used to contextualize performance.

Audit Level

Evidence level used to define Factor C.

Evaluation Sequence

PRMS Score Dimensions

Complete each dimension using measured values, documented evidence, or verified operational data. The final score is calculated from the weighted contribution of all dimensions.

D1 · Energy

Production, consumption, and autonomy.

D2 · Water

Rainwater capture, efficient use, and reuse.

D3 · Waste

Reduction, separation, and recovery.

D4 · Comfort

Thermal, lighting, and indoor air quality.

D5 · Economy

Return on investment and economic performance.

D6 · Resilience

Operational continuity under extreme conditions.

D7 · Carbon

Dynamic carbon footprint and avoided emissions.

D8 · Operation & Data

Monitoring, maintenance, management, and data traceability.

D1 · Energy

Evaluates photovoltaic generation, electricity consumption, grid import/export, installed capacity, energy autonomy, self-consumption, and energy intensity.

D2 · Water

Evaluates potable water demand, rainwater capture, greywater reuse, sanitary discharge, own water coverage, and reduction against baseline consumption.

D3 · Waste

Evaluates total waste generation, recycling, composting, recovery rate, landfill reduction, and per-capita waste generation.

D4 · Comfort

Evaluates indoor temperature, hours outside thermal comfort, daylight, indoor CO₂, relative humidity, PM2.5, and acoustic conditions.

D5 · Economy

Evaluates initial investment, annual savings, operation and maintenance costs, payback, ROI, IRR, and net present value.

D6 · Resilience

Evaluates electrical autonomy, water autonomy, redundancy of critical systems, annual continuity, recovery time, and active energy sources.

D7 · Carbon

Evaluates operational emissions, avoided emissions, carbon intensity, net carbon balance, and grid emission factor.

D8 · Operation & Data

Evaluates IoT energy sensors, IoT water sensors, continuous records, historical data storage, external verification, and operational data management.

Output

Final PRMS Score Report

Once all dimensions are completed, the calculator generates a global PRMS Score, a metabolic certification level, weighted dimension results, Factor C, and a printable performance report.
