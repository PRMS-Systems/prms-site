Operation & Data Performance Module

D8 Operation & Data evaluates the digital maturity of the project. This dimension measures whether the building can monitor, record, store, verify, and manage operational information through continuous data systems.

A building with verified IoT sensors, continuous records, historical storage, and external verification is more governable, auditable, and optimizable than a building based only on estimates or periodic utility bills.

Data Reliability Logic

D8 is included as a score dimension because operational intelligence is part of the metabolic performance of a building. Factor C remains the separate verification multiplier applied to the global PRMS Score.
