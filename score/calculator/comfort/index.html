Comfort Performance Module

D4 Comfort evaluates the environmental quality experienced by occupants. This dimension measures indoor temperature, hours outside thermal comfort, daylight availability, indoor CO₂ concentration, relative humidity, PM2.5, and acoustic conditions.

The objective is to determine whether the building supports health, comfort, habitability, and stable indoor environmental performance.
