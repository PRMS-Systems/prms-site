Economic Performance Module

D5 Economy evaluates the financial performance of the project as a metabolic asset. This dimension measures initial investment, annual energy savings, annual water savings, operation and maintenance costs, simple payback, annual ROI, project IRR, and net present value.

The objective is to determine whether the system produces measurable economic value, reduces operating costs, and supports long-term financial viability.
