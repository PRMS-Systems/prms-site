Carbon Performance Module

D7 Carbon evaluates the operational carbon profile of the project. This dimension measures operational emissions, avoided emissions from photovoltaic generation, carbon intensity, net carbon balance, and the emission factor of the electricity grid.

The objective is to determine whether the project reduces, offsets, or neutralizes operational carbon emissions through energy performance and low-carbon operation.
