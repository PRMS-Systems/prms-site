Evaluation Framework

PRMS Score
Metabolic Performance Evaluation

PRMS Score is a quantitative evaluation framework developed by PRMS Architecture Lab to assess the integral metabolic performance of buildings through measurable indicators, weighted dimensions, verification levels, and operational data.

What PRMS Score measures

PRMS Score evaluates buildings as metabolic systems. Instead of assessing architecture only by form, function, or efficiency, it measures how a project produces, consumes, stores, reuses, verifies, and manages critical flows over time.

Core purpose

To provide a measurable, comparable, and verifiable performance score for residential and small-scale architectural systems.

The 8 PRMS Score Dimensions

Start · Project Data

Project identification, typology, climate zone, audit level, and Factor C.

D1 · Energy

Production, consumption, and autonomy.

D2 · Water

Rainwater capture, efficient use, and reuse.

D3 · Waste

Reduction, separation, and recovery.

D4 · Comfort

Thermal, lighting, and indoor air quality.

D5 · Economy

Return on investment and economic performance.

D6 · Resilience

Operational continuity under extreme conditions.

D7 · Carbon

Dynamic carbon footprint and avoided emissions.

D8 · Operation & Data

Monitoring, maintenance, management, and data traceability.

Final · Results Dashboard

Global PRMS Score, Factor C, dimension scores, returns, and certification level.

Certification scale

The global score ranges from 0 to 100 and classifies each project into a metabolic performance level.

Factor C

PRMS Score includes a confidence multiplier called Factor C. This factor adjusts the final score according to the reliability of the evidence used in the evaluation.
